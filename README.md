# Tone Adjustments

**Precise control over every part of your tonal range.**

---

## What it does

Tone Adjustments gives you seven independent sliders that let you shape the light in a photo without touching anything else. Darken only the deep shadows while leaving midtones untouched, pull back bright highlights without losing detail — each adjustment targets exactly the luminance range you aim at and fades out naturally on either side.

---

## Features

- **Five tone band sliders** — independently adjust Blacks, Shadows, Midtones, Highlights, and Whites
- **Exposure** — global brightness in stops (±2 EV)
- **Contrast** — global contrast around mid-grey
- All adjustments blend smoothly: raising Shadows does not clip into Blacks or Midtones
- Fully non-destructive — changes are stored in your edit history and can be removed or modified at any time
- Live preview updates as you drag any slider

---

## How to use

1. Open a photo in PhotoDewey
2. Open the **Tone Adjustments** panel in the sidebar
3. Drag any slider — positive values brighten, negative values darken that portion of the tonal range
4. Use **Exposure** for an overall brightness correction and **Contrast** to add punch or create a soft look
5. All edits are added to your history — undo or remove individual adjustments at any time
